About Me

About Peter Oduro Twumasi Senior

I chose welding and fabrication because I want to fuse metals together and contribute to protecting lives through safety and sound structural welds. I enjoy the process of welding, especially the precision and skill involved in bringing separate pieces of metal together.

“Welding is not just a skill; welding is a battle.”

My goal is to become a professional welder and an NDT Inspector, combining practical welding experience with inspection and quality assurance.

Projects

Selected Projects

The Forger’s Hands

DTI Capstone Metal Artwork

Theme: Bridging the Gap Between Academia and Industry

A metal artwork representing the relationship between Academia and Industry. The two hands represent Academic Institutions and Industry; the arc in the middle represents the connection between them.

Inspired by the idea that the standards and training provided by schools should meet the demands of industry. As industries evolve, academic institutions must adapt and follow their changing needs.

The artwork was meticulously designed and fabricated to communicate the importance of collaboration between educational institutions and industry in preparing students with relevant skills for the workplace.
